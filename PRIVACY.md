# Privacy Policy for Facecard

_Last updated: August 12, 2026_

**Short version: Facecard keeps everything on your device. It has no accounts,
no servers, and no internet connection. Nothing you put into the app is ever
collected, transmitted, sold, or shared.**

## Information Facecard handles

Everything you add to Facecard — people's names, pronouns, notes, group tags,
photos, and your practice scores — is stored **only on your own device**, in the
app's private storage. This information never leaves your phone. The developer
cannot see it, and no third party receives it.

Facecard does **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, crash
telemetry, contacts, location, or any other personal data.

## Camera and photo permissions

When you choose to add a picture to a person, Facecard asks for permission to
use your **camera** or **photo library**. These permissions are used for one
purpose only: to let you pick or take a photo to add to your deck. When you add
a photo, a copy is saved into the app's private storage on your device. Photos
are never uploaded anywhere.

You can decline or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings;
the rest of the app will keep working.

## No accounts, no network

Facecard has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. The app contains no
code that sends your information over the internet.

## Data sharing

None. Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing to share with
anyone — no advertisers, no analytics providers, no other companies.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you choose to remove it:

- Delete an individual person in the app to remove that person and their photos.
- **Uninstalling Facecard permanently deletes all of its data** from your device.

There is no cloud backup, so uninstalling cannot be undone — export any data you
want to keep first (when that feature is available).

## Children's privacy

Facecard does not collect any personal information from anyone, including
children. All data stays on the device under the control of whoever uses it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated policy will
be published here with a new "Last updated" date.
